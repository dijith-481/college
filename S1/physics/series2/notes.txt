bands become wider as we move towards highier energy states because energy of electron becomes larger and region become wider for both outer occupied and unoccupied subshells.
an electron in a solid can only occupy energy that falls within these energy bands.
it depends on structure of solid if bands doesn't overlap then the region become forbidden bands or energy gaps.
if adjacent bands overlap then electron possses a continous distribution of allowed energy levels.

based on energy gap solids can be classified into isulator,semiconductor and conductor
for insulators the energy gap is larger they are between 3 to 6eV between valence and conduction band.
only few electrons from the valence band reaches the conduction band even if we thermally excite the atom or apply electric field also pauli exclusion principle prevents the electron movement in filled band.
they are poor conductors of electricity.
ZnO, AgCl, Diamond, quartz

for semiconductors the energy gap is between 0.1 to 1eV so they can move from valence band to condction band by thermal excitation or by applying electric field.
so a free electron current is obtained as few electrons are available in the empty band.
silicon, germanium.
there also exist another mechanism that causes electric current the empty places in top of the  filled band called holes they behave as positive electrons and cause the generation of electric current electrons below the holes attain enough energy and obtain the hole due to applied electric field causes theh generation of electric current.

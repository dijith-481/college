### **Combined Core Points: Organizational Behavior & Business Communication**

---

### **Part 1: Organizational Behavior (OB) - People at Work**

- **What is OB?** The study of how people and groups act at work, used to make a company more effective.

- **Personality:** Your unique style of behaving.

  - **Shaped By:** Heredity (genes) and Environment (culture, upbringing).
  - **Key Theory (Big Five - OCEAN):**
    - **O**penness: Are you creative?
    - **C**onscientiousness: Are you organized & reliable? (Most linked to job success).
    - **E**xtraversion: Are you outgoing?
    - **A**greeableness: Are you cooperative?
    - **N**euroticism: Are you emotionally stable?

- **Perception:** How you see and interpret the world. Your "reality."

- **Learning & Reinforcement:**

  - **Learning:** A change in behavior from experience.
  - **Reinforcement Theory:** Behavior is controlled by its consequences.
    - **Positive Reinforcement:** Give a reward to _increase_ a good behavior.
    - **Negative Reinforcement:** Remove something bad to _increase_ a good behavior.
    - **Punishment:** Give something bad to _decrease_ a bad behavior.

- **Group Dynamics:**

  - **5 Stages of Group Formation (FSNPA):**
    1.  **F**orming: Meet & greet.
    2.  **S**torming: Conflict & fighting for roles.
    3.  **N**orming: Agree on rules, start to bond.
    4.  **P**erforming: Work effectively as a team.
    5.  **A**djourning: Group breaks up.

- **Stress Management:**
  - **Consequences of Stress:** Can be physical (illness), psychological (anxiety), and behavioral (low productivity).
  - **Management:** Handled by both the **Individual** (time management, healthy life) and the **Organization** (work-life balance, support).

### **Part 2: Business Communication - Sharing Information**

- **What is it?** The process of sharing information to achieve business goals.

- **Communication Process:** Sender -> Message -> Channel -> Receiver.

  - **Barrier:** Anything that blocks the message (e.g., noise, language, mistrust).

- **The 7 C’s of Communication (Crucial for effective messages):**

  1.  **Clear:** Is it easy to understand?
  2.  **Concise:** Is it short and to the point?
  3.  **Concrete:** Is it specific and factual?
  4.  **Correct:** Is it accurate and error-free?
  5.  **Coherent:** Does it make logical sense?
  6.  **Complete:** Does it contain all needed info?
  7.  **Courteous:** Is it polite and respectful?

- **Types of Communication:**

  - **Verbal:** Spoken words.
  - **Non-Verbal:** Body language (often more powerful than words).
  - **Written:** Emails, reports, memos.

- **Soft Skills:** Personal skills needed to work well with others (e.g., teamwork, empathy, leadership).

- **Business Etiquette:** The unwritten rules of being professional (be on time, dress appropriately, be respectful).

- **Listening:** The most critical communication skill.
  - **Importance:** Builds trust, prevents errors, and helps solve problems.
  - **Key Types:**
    - **Informational:** Listening to learn.
    - **Critical:** Listening to evaluate or judge.
    - **Empathetic:** Listening to understand someone's feelings.

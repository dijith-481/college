free electron theory
free electron theroy formulated by the drude and leter expanded by the lorentz it is based on the electron gas model
it assumes the electrons at ordinary temperature move in metals at random direction at a speed of 10^5 m/s during these movement they collide with the atoms or lattice   and with themselves they have no practical contribution to the current is zero.
when an electric potential is given to the metal electrons accelerate in the opposite direction with an average velocity known as drift velocity. In thermal equilibrium electrons are assumed to follow maxwell-boltzmann distribution
